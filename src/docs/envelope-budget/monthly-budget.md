## The three columns

Every category row shows three numbers, once per visible month:

- **Assigned** — the dollars you committed to the category that month. Editable; press Enter or click away to save. The grid is fully keyboard-driven: Tab reaches it once, arrows move between categories and month columns, Enter or a digit starts the edit, Escape cancels.
- **Outflow** — the sum of transactions in this category that month. Outflows are negative; inflows show as positive.
- **Available** — `carry-in + assigned + activity`. Green when funded, gray at zero, red when overspent. Click any category name or Available pill to open the inspector, where the carry-in breakdown, notes, goal, planned cut, and recent activity live.

## Multiple months at once

The 1 / 2 / 3 picker in the toolbar shows up to three month columns side by side (on wide screens; phones stay single-month). The leftmost column is the anchor month; the extra columns run forward so you can budget ahead. Editing a cell writes to its own column's month.

## Ready to Assign

The stat at the top of [Budget](/app/budget/) shows the dollars that have entered an on-budget account but haven't been given a job yet. Click it: the month overview breaks the number down — inflow, everything assigned so far, and overspending lost (overspending in a prior month doesn't follow the category; it comes out of Ready to Assign the next month). Every overspent category is listed there with a one-click cover from the category holding the most available.

## Quick budget

Select a category and the inspector offers one-click fills, each showing its amount before you press it:

- **Underfunded (goal)** — add exactly what the goal still needs this month.
- **Last month's assigned** — copy last month's plan.
- **Last month's spending** — assign whatever you actually spent here last month.
- **Average of last 3 months** — smooth out one-off swings.

Each fill is a single undo step.

## Planned cuts

Mark any category with a reduction target — a dollar amount per month, or a percent of its baseline (the average of the three months before you planned the cut; the baseline stays frozen, so succeeding doesn't move the goalposts). Optionally name the goal the savings are for. The row wears a `-$X/mo` badge, and the month overview totals the portfolio: planned and realized savings, the annualized figure, the share of your income, and your age of money — how old the dollars you spend are — today and where the cuts would push it.
